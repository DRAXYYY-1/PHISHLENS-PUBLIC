How to Use PhishLens AI Analyzer
Step 1: Open the Website
Visit the PhishLens AI Analyzer web application:

https://phishlens-ai.hatchable.site

Step 2: Request Access
The application is managed by the project administrator. Contact the administrator and request verification and approval if you do not have access.

Step 3: Enter the Web Interface
After your access is approved, open the website and navigate to the email analysis interface.

Step 4: Submit an Email
Enter or paste the suspicious email content into the available input field. Include relevant details such as the sender, subject, and email body when supported by the interface.

Step 5: Start the Analysis
Click the Analyze Email button to begin processing the submitted email.

Step 6: Review the Risk Report
Wait for the analysis to finish. Review the generated report, including the identified phishing indicators, potential threats, and risk assessment.

Step 7: Review the CERT Email Draft
If a CERT incident email draft is generated, review its contents and correct any inaccurate details before using it for reporting.

Step 8: Take Appropriate Action
If the email appears suspicious, avoid clicking its links or opening its attachments. Follow your organization's security procedures and report the incident to the appropriate cybersecurity response team when necessary.

Important Notes
